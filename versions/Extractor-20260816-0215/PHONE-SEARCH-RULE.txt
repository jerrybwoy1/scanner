Mandatory phone search behavior

For every valid US phone number, automatically search all of these forms:
1. (AAA) BBB-CCCC
2. AAA-BBB-CCCC
3. AAA BBB CCCC
4. AAABBBCCCC
5. +1 AAA BBB CCCC

The first two formats are mandatory and covered by regression tests. This applies to manual searches, workbook rows, and newly discovered numbers that trigger follow-up research.
